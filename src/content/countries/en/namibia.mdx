## The World's Oldest Desert and Second-Least-Populated Country

Namibia is one of the world's **least densely populated countries** — just **3 million people in 825,000 km²** (about 3 people per km²), second only to Mongolia. Most of the country is desert — the **Namib** (claimed by geologists to be **the world's oldest desert** at ~55-80 million years) on the Atlantic coast, and the **Kalahari** in the east.

The country's landscapes are among the most spectacular on Earth — **Sossusvlei's** red dunes (some reaching 325 m, among the world's tallest), the **Skeleton Coast** (haunted by shipwrecks where cold Atlantic currents meet scorching desert), **Etosha National Park** (a vast salt pan and wildlife reserve), and **Fish River Canyon** (Africa's largest canyon, 2nd-largest in the world after the Grand Canyon).

Namibia was the site of the **first genocide of the 20th century** — German colonial forces massacred **50,000-100,000 Herero and Nama people** (1904-1908), 80% of the Herero population and 50% of the Nama. **Germany formally recognised the genocide in 2021** and agreed to €1.1 billion in reparations.

The country was colonised by Germany (1884-1915), then administered by **South Africa (1915-1990)** under the apartheid system — becoming the **last African country to gain independence** in 1990.

## A Brief History

**Herero, Nama, Damara, Ovambo peoples**. German South-West Africa 1884-1915. **German genocide of Herero and Nama 1904-1908**. South African administration 1915-1990. **SWAPO liberation war** 1966-1989. **Independence in 1990**.

## Geography and Climate

Namibia covers **825,615 km²**. Namib desert west, Kalahari east, central plateau. Climate: arid.

## Culture, Language and Religion

**English** is official. Religion: approximately 90% Christian. Major ethnic groups: Ovambo (50%), Kavango, Herero, Damara, Nama, San (Bushmen).

## The Economy

Namibia has an **upper-middle-income economy** (~$13 billion GDP). Mining (uranium — 3rd-largest global producer, diamonds), fishing, and tourism dominate.

## UNESCO Sites

Namibia has **2 UNESCO World Heritage Sites**: **Twyfelfontein** (ancient rock engravings) and **Namib Sand Sea**.

## Travel Guide

**Entry**: Visa-free for many nationalities. **Best seasons**: May-October. Self-drive tourism is excellent — roads are good and distances manageable.

## Surprising Facts

1. **Namibia is the world's 2nd-least densely populated country** — just 3 people per km².
2. **The Namib Desert** is the world's oldest desert — approximately 55-80 million years.
3. **Sossusvlei's dunes** reach up to 325 m — among the world's tallest.
4. **Germany's 1904-1908 genocide** of the Herero and Nama was the 20th century's first; Germany formally recognised it in 2021.
5. **Namibia has Africa's largest free-roaming cheetah population** — about 3,500 (1/3 of the world total).
6. **Fish River Canyon** is the world's 2nd-largest canyon after the Grand Canyon.

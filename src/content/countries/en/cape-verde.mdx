## Africa's Most Stable Democracy

Cape Verde (officially Cabo Verde) is a 10-island archipelago approximately 570 km west of Senegal in the Atlantic. The country has achieved something rare in Africa — **consistent democratic governance, peaceful transfers of power, and strong human development indicators**. It is regularly ranked among Africa's **top 3 countries for democracy and press freedom**, comparable to Mauritius and Botswana.

The islands were **uninhabited before Portuguese discovery** in 1456 — Portuguese settlement from 1462 created the first African colony with a large European-descended population. The islands became a major **Atlantic slave-trading hub** (Cidade Velha — the original capital — was the first permanent European settlement in the tropics and a transit point for approximately half a million enslaved Africans).

Cape Verdean culture is a unique **Afro-Portuguese creole** — the Cape Verdean **morna** music genre (especially through **Cesária Évora**, "the Barefoot Diva", 1941-2011) became globally recognised. The islands have a large **diaspora** — there are more Cape Verdeans abroad (USA, Portugal, Netherlands, France) than on the islands themselves.

Tourism — particularly to **Sal** and **Boa Vista** (beach destinations) and **Santo Antão** (hiking) — is the dominant economic sector.

## A Brief History

Uninhabited before Portuguese discovery 1456. Portuguese colony from 1462. Major slave trade transit point. **Independence in 1975** after Portuguese Carnation Revolution. Democratic multiparty system from 1990.

## Geography and Climate

Cape Verde covers **4,033 km²** across 10 islands. Volcanic, mountainous. Climate: arid-semiarid, moderated by the Atlantic.

## Culture, Language and Religion

**Portuguese** is official; **Cape Verdean Creole (Kriolu)** is widely spoken. Religion: approximately 77% Catholic. Major cultural features include morna music and Creole cuisine.

## The Economy

Cape Verde has a **lower-middle-income economy** (~$2.8 billion GDP). Tourism, remittances, and fishing are the main sectors. The country relies on imports for most food (arid islands).

## UNESCO Sites

Cape Verde has **1 UNESCO World Heritage Site**: **Cidade Velha, Historic Centre of Ribeira Grande** on Santiago island.

## Travel Guide

**Entry**: Visa-on-arrival / ETA system. **Best seasons**: November-May.

## Surprising Facts

1. **Cape Verde ranks among Africa's top 3 democracies** alongside Mauritius and Botswana.
2. **The islands were uninhabited** before Portuguese discovery in 1456.
3. **Cidade Velha** was the first permanent European settlement in the tropics.
4. **Cesária Évora** — "the Barefoot Diva" — put Cape Verdean morna music on the world stage.
5. **More Cape Verdeans live abroad** than on the islands themselves — diaspora 1+ million, resident 550,000.
6. **Fogo Island's active volcano** (Pico do Fogo, 2,829 m) last erupted in 2014, destroying several villages.
